How to annotate your SnapGene map
Feature labels: use [name] for a fragment that is fixed across constructs and {name} for a fragment that varies across constructs. The core overlapping pUC ori or ori pUC is automatically the backbone. The order of cores on the circular map is the assembly order. Add one (name) or - feature at each core boundary; it marks where that junction may be placed. Each junction region must be at least 4 bp. Other ordinary annotations are ignored. The detected backbone is output as PCR; other cores as synthesis fragments.
